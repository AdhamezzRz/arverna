06 · The sticker pack

A little gift in every order

Kiss-cut sheets · girly · collectible

Sheet 1 · The icons

A-Bow, iced latte, mini bag, kiss, pearl heart, perfume, gold hoops. No words, so every girl uses them.

Sheet 2 · Say it in English

"main character energy", "it's giving ARVERNA", "outfit repeater & proud", "treat yourself, habibti".

Sheet 3 · قوليها بالعربي

The things girls actually say: من وين؟ · حبيبتي · شو ألبس؟ · يلا نطلع · بنت أرفيرنا · طلبت كمان مرة

How it works

  • Every orderOne A6 sheet in a cream envelope that says "a little gift for you".
  • Rotate monthlyA new sheet each month, so repeat customers get something new and want the full set.
  • Mix the languagesAlternate icon, English and Arabic sheets. The Arabic one will be the most posted.
  • Print specGlossy waterproof vinyl, kiss-cut, 2–3 mm white border, @arvernacom on the backing paper. Strong enough for laptops, phone cases and tumblers.

07 · How stickers spread

Stickers that travel

Share · collect · post
Bestie split
Collector card
The gift moment
Where they end up
Referral

One for your bestie

A heart split in two: "BEST" for her, "IE" for her friend. Print her referral code on the back of the "IE" half, so giving it away brings a new customer.

Loyalty

Collect them all

A collector card with 12 spots, one exclusive sticker per drop. A full card unlocks a reward, for example the A-Bow bag charm.

Unboxing

Film the envelope

The sticker envelope sits on top under the ribbon, so it's the first thing on camera in every unboxing video.

UGC

#ArvernaStickers

Ask girls to post where they stuck theirs: laptop, phone case, tumbler, planner. Repost the best and send a bonus sheet.
