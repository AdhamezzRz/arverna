The brand storyFor the About page, the launch Reel and the order card
The girl they call a gazelle

In the hills around Hebron lives the mountain gazelle. Light on her feet, quick, alert, impossible not to watch.

Here, we've always used her name as the best compliment. When a girl walks in and the room turns, someone says it: «يا غزالة».

ARVERNA was born in the same city, for that same girl. She has a full day: campus at 8, coffee at 4, a night out at 9. She saves looks from everywhere and wants them now, not in three weeks.

So we bring them to her door in two to three days. New pieces every Thursday. Real fits, honest prices, and a friend called Reem who always knows what's new.

You were born graceful.
We just dress it.

بجبال الخليل بتعيش الغزالة الجبلية. خفيفة، سريعة، منتبهة، وما بتقدر ما تطّلع عليها.

وعنّا، اسمها هو أحلى مدح. لمّا بنت تفوت وكل العيون تلتفت، دايمًا في حدا بيقول: «يا غزالة».

أرڤيرنا انولدت بنفس المدينة، لنفس البنت. يومها مليان: جامعة الساعة ٨، قهوة الساعة ٤، وطلعة الساعة ٩. بتحفظ لوكات من كل مكان، وبدها إياها هلّق، مش بعد ثلاث أسابيع.

فإحنا بنوصّلها لباب بيتها خلال ٢–٣ أيام. قطع جديدة كل خميس، مقاسات حقيقية، أسعار واضحة، وصاحبة اسمها ريم بتعرف دايمًا شو الجديد.

Why the gazelleThe icon
Four reasons it's a gazelle
01

It's from here

The mountain gazelle is native to the hills of Palestine, around Hebron included. Our symbol comes from our own landscape, not a stock idea.

من أرضنا
02

It's our compliment

«يا غزالة» is what girls here already say to each other. Every time someone says it, they say our brand.

من كلامنا
03

It moves like her

Light, quick, confident. That's everyday fashion: clothes that keep up with her day.

زي حركتها
04

It's rare

The mountain gazelle is now endangered. It makes the mark precious, and gives the brand a real cause if you want one later.

نادرة
And the details

The closed eye with long lashes makes it hers, not a hunting emblem. One sweeping horn reads as motion. The neck line falls like fabric. She looks calm and sure of herself, which is exactly how we want customers to feel in our clothes.

Logo rulesHow to use it
Three versions, one gazelle
Stacked · mainGazelle centred above the wordmark. Instagram profile picture, tags, boxes, website footer, campaign posters.
HorizontalFor wide, short spaces: website header, email, receipts, the shop sign.
Gazelle aloneCorner mark on photos, tissue seal, favicon, embroidery.
WordmarkThe ARVERNA wordmark is unchanged. Never retype it in a font; always use the file.
Shopping bagsBags carry the wordmark only, no gazelle.
Space and sizeKeep empty space around the logo at least the height of the letter A. Smallest stacked logo: 110 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print.
Don'tStretch it, rotate it, add shadows or outlines, put it on busy photos, or recolour it outside the palette.

Glossغلوس
The pop. Reem, community posts, "new".#F2A2BE · 6%

A finish, not a fill. Your foil logo, the icon charm, zips and hardware.foil / metal · 5%

Why it fits. Same energy as the high-street labels she already shops, but warmer and ownable. Their feeds are black and white; yours is coffee and denim.

Why it's practical. Most of what you sell is brown, beige, cream and blue denim. Product photos match the brand without editing.

Pairs that always work. Oat on mocha. Espresso on oat. Espresso on denim. Espresso on gloss. Chrome on mocha or espresso.

Rules. One accent per post: denim or gloss, never both. No pure black, no pure white, no red sale banners.

RulesCheck every post against this
Do and don't
Always
✓Real collection photos first, Reem second
✓Reem as a real toy in real places, shot on a phone
✓Steady, smooth movement (never shaky), light grain, warm grade
✓Arabic first in captions, English mixed in
✓Prices, sizes and model height on every product post
✓Mocha, oat, espresso; denim or gloss as the one accent
✓Say «يا غزالة»; end Reels on «غزالة من يومك»

✕Supplier photos or another brand's shoot
✕Smooth glossy CGI Reem on the grid
✕Pure black, pure white, red "SALE!!" banners
✕Retyping the wordmark in a font
✕The gazelle on shopping bags (wordmark only)
✕Reem covering or replacing the clothes
✕Silver horns or old icon versions
