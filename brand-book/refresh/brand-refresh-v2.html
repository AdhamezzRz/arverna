01 — Icon AThe Gazelle A
A letter with
a gazelle inside

Two smooth horns meet to make the A of ARVERNA. Between them is her face, with two leaf-shaped ears. The spikes are gone; every line is a curve.

Why it's feminine

Tapered strokes, soft ears and a slim face. It reads like jewellery, not armour.

Two readings

First you see an A. Then you see the gazelle. That second look is what makes people remember it.

Best for

Profile picture, favicon, tags, embroidery. It stays clear when it is small.

02 — Icon BThe Profile
Her, in
four strokes

A gazelle in profile: one long horn, a leaf ear, a slim face and a closed eye with lashes. Calm and confident, like a beauty-brand emblem.

Why it's feminine

The lashes and the sweeping horn. It is the most elegant of all the options.

Trade-off

It is a picture, not a letter. And the fine lines fade below about 32 px, so it needs a bolder small-size version.

Best for

Big uses: shopping bags, tissue paper, jewellery, back-neck prints, campaign posters.

03 — A or BRecommendation
You don't have to choose

They are the same gazelle, seen from the front and from the side. Use them as a pair, each where it is strongest.

The icon
Gazelle A

The main brand icon. It carries the letter, so it can stand alone without the wordmark.

Use on: profile picture, website favicon, swing tags, labels, embroidery, the corner mark on every photo, stickers.

The emblem
The Profile

The decorative mark, used large and always together with the wordmark or the icon.

Use on: shopping bags, tissue paper, box lids, jewellery and charms, campaign posters, the back-neck print.

If you want only one: Gazelle A. It works at every size and is the easier one to protect as a trademark with your name.

04 — Reem in 2DModel sheet
Same Reem from every side
Turnaround — front, three-quarter, side, back
Expressions — eight moods for stickers and replies

What this is. The sheet an animator or illustrator works from, so Reem looks the same in every post.

Fixed features. Two silver horns, long drooping ears, cream face mask, three lashes, pink cheeks.

What changes. Only the outfit and the mood. Her body and face never change.

Sticker pack. The eight expressions are ready to become WhatsApp and Instagram stickers.
