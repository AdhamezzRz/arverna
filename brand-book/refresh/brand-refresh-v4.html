01 — The hornBefore / after
Same gazelle. A real horn.
Before — one long sweeping horn
After · recommended — two ringed horns
Option — one ringed horn

What changed. Only the horn. The ear, the closed eye with lashes, the muzzle and the long neck line are the ones you chose.

Why it looks real now. Gazelle horns grow from just above the eye, rise, curve back, and hook forward at the tip, with rings along the front. Seen from the side you see two.

Pick one. Two horns is the most natural and balanced. One horn is lighter and simpler at very small sizes.
