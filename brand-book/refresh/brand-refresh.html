Why it fits. Same energy as the high-street labels she already shops, but warmer and ownable. Their feeds are black and white; yours is coffee and denim.

Why it's practical. Most of what you sell is brown, beige, cream and blue denim. Product photos match the brand without editing.

Pairs that always work. Oat on mocha. Espresso on oat. Espresso on denim. Espresso on gloss. Chrome on mocha or espresso.

Rules. One accent per post: denim or gloss, never both. No pure black, no pure white, no red sale banners.

02 — The iconThe Horn A
An A made of
gazelle horns

Two ridged horns lean in and meet. Add a crossbar and it is the A of ARVERNA. The curved, pointed shape is deliberately the opposite of the wide, flat wordmark, so the two sit together without competing.

Why a gazelle

In Arabic, calling a girl a gazelle is the classic compliment. يا غزالة is how she already talks to her friends.

Why it's yours

The mountain gazelle lives in the hills of Palestine. It is local, graceful and fast, like the brand.

Why it works

It is a letter, so it never needs explaining, and it holds at 16 px or as a metal charm.

03 — The characterReem
Reem
ريم

Reem is an Arabic girl's name that means gazelle. She is ARVERNA's character: a collectible figure with chrome horns who wears every new drop before anyone else.

WhoThe stylish friend. Confident, funny, never mean. Obsessed with denim and iced coffee.
LookMocha, with chrome horns like the foil logo, long lashes, a gloss-pink mini bag. Always fully dressed in ARVERNA.
VoiceShort, warm, in dialect. «هاي يا غزالة، شوفي شو نزل»
JobAnnounces drops, answers size questions, stars in Reels and stickers, and exists as a plush charm girls collect.
NeverBabyish, sarcastic about customers, or dressed in something you don't sell.
Why a character

Collectible characters on bags are what girls are buying and posting right now. A character can also post daily, never gets tired, and stays yours, unlike an influencer.

05 — InstagramThe grid
The
checkerboard

Brand tiles and product photos alternate. From far away the profile reads as one mocha, denim and oat pattern. Up close, every second post is something to buy.

01Alternate. Brand tile, product photo, brand tile. Never two graphics side by side.
02Rotate the colours. Denim, mocha, oat, gloss, espresso. No colour twice in a row.
03Mark every photo. A small Horn A in the bottom corner, so reposts still carry the brand.
04Choose photos by colour. Brown, beige, cream and denim pieces first. Loud colours go to Stories.
05Reem once per row. Enough to be the face, not so much that the feed becomes a toy account.
From far away

Colour blocks are brand tiles. Dark blocks are product photos. The pattern stays the same however far she scrolls.

Posting rhythm

Post in pairs: one brand tile and one photo, so the checkerboard never breaks.
