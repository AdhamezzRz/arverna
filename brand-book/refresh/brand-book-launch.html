02 — The brand storyFor the About page, the launch Reel and the order card
The girl they call a gazelle

In the hills around Hebron lives the mountain gazelle. Light on her feet, quick, alert, impossible not to watch.

Here, we've always used her name as the best compliment. When a girl walks in and the room turns, someone says it: «يا غزالة».

ARVERNA was born in the same city, for that same girl. She has a full day: campus at 8, coffee at 4, a night out at 9. She saves looks from everywhere and wants them now, not in three weeks.

So we bring them to her door in two to three days. New pieces every Thursday. Real fits, honest prices, and a friend called Reem who always knows what's new.

You were born graceful.
We just dress it.

بجبال الخليل بتعيش الغزالة الجبلية. خفيفة، سريعة، منتبهة، وما بتقدر ما تطّلع عليها.

وعنّا، اسمها هو أحلى مدح. لمّا بنت تفوت وكل العيون تلتفت، دايمًا في حدا بيقول: «يا غزالة».

أرڤيرنا انولدت بنفس المدينة، لنفس البنت. يومها مليان: جامعة الساعة ٨، قهوة الساعة ٤، وطلعة الساعة ٩. بتحفظ لوكات من كل مكان، وبدها إياها هلّق، مش بعد ثلاث أسابيع.

فإحنا بنوصّلها لباب بيتها خلال ٢–٣ أيام. قطع جديدة كل خميس، مقاسات حقيقية، أسعار واضحة، وصاحبة اسمها ريم بتعرف دايمًا شو الجديد.

03 — Why the gazelleThe icon
Four reasons it's a gazelle
01

It's from here

The mountain gazelle is native to the hills of Palestine, around Hebron included. Our symbol comes from our own landscape, not a stock idea.

من أرضنا
02

It's our compliment

«يا غزالة» is what girls here already say to each other. Every time someone says it, they say our brand.

من كلامنا
03

It moves like her

Light, quick, confident. That's everyday fashion: clothes that keep up with her day.

زي حركتها
04

It's rare

The mountain gazelle is now endangered. It makes the mark precious, and gives the brand a real cause if you want one later.

نادرة
And the details

The closed eye with long lashes makes it hers, not a hunting emblem. One sweeping horn reads as motion. The neck line falls like fabric. She looks calm and sure of herself, which is exactly how we want customers to feel in our clothes.

07 — The launchThree posts · one week
Three posts. One week.

Tease her on Tuesday, reveal her on Thursday at 8 PM like a drop, and show her in our clothes on Saturday. Everything else (the shadow, the eye, the box) goes in Stories, so the grid stays clean.

Post 1 · Tue · 8 PM · Reel
Tease

Only her horns peek in and duck away. Nobody knows who she is yet.

Post 2 · Thu · 8 PM · Carousel
Reveal

The reveal animation as slide 1, then her name, her city, her style.

Post 3 · Sat · 8 PM · Reel
Her wardrobe

One spin and her hoodie becomes our leather jacket. Plus a giveaway.

Why only three. Three posts make one complete row on the grid: the whole story sits together at the top of the profile.

Why Thursday 8 PM. It's already your drop time. Reem arrives the way every collection does, so she's linked to new clothes from day one.

Stories fill the gaps. Daily clues and a countdown in Stories keep people curious between posts without crowding the feed.
