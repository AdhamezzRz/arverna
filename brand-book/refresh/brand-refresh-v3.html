How to start posting this
Step 1
Set the look

Save one photo preset for the warm grade, and the templates for the price tag, pills and corner mark.

Step 2
Lock Reem

A 3D artist builds one final Reem model, so every pose and every mini outfit looks identical.

Step 3
Shoot for formats

For each drop, shoot one street look, one studio look and one close-up detail. That covers every format.

Step 4
Post in threes

Three posts per drop week, one complete row at a time, plus the Thursday Story countdown.
