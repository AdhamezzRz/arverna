01 · The name

She who stands in front

ARVERNA is a Latin word. It means a woman of the Arverni, the Gallic people who held the mountains of central France and faced down Julius Caesar at Gergovia in 52 BC. Their name carried a promise the brand can keep.

ARE–Celtic: before, in front of
–VERN–Celtic: the alder tree
–ALatin: she, the feminine
ARVERNAShe who stands in front

Linguists reconstruct the tribe's name as are-uernos, "those before the alders," usually read as "the foremost ones." The brand line uses that reading.

Tree one · Auvergne

The alder. The Arverni stood in front of it.

The name is about position: first in line, first to speak, first to try the new thing.

Tree two · Hebron

The oak. Al-Khalil welcomed strangers under it.

Hebron is named for Ibrahim al-Khalil, "the friend." His old oak at Mamre is where, the story goes, strangers were welcomed as friends.

Two trees, one woman. She stands in front, and she keeps the door open. That's the whole brand in one line: confidence that brings people in rather than shutting them out.

02 · The story

The brand story

Use it in full on the About page, on the packaging insert and in the first Reel of the new era. Use one line of it everywhere else.

Two thousand years ago, in the mountains of Gaul, there lived a people called the Arverni. Their name meant those who stand in front. A woman of that people was called Arverna.

Today she lives in Hebron.

Her city is built from limestone that holds the warmth of the day. Its souq is older than anyone can remember. Grapes ripen on the rooftops every August. Its oldest tree, the story goes, sheltered strangers who were welcomed as friends, and the city still carries that name: al-Khalil, the friend.

She learned two things here. Stand in front: be the first to arrive, the first to try, the first to say yes to something new. And keep the door open, because the best outfit is the one your friends ask to borrow.

ARVERNA dresses her for the life she actually lives. Sets that go from a 9 a.m. lecture to a 9 p.m. engagement. Trench coats for Hebron's cold mornings. Lace for Eid. Denim for everything in between.

We take the best of what the world is wearing and cut it to her rhythm.

Hebron-born. Worn everywhere.

Why "Arwa"

Arwa is a familiar Arabic name that opens with the same sound as ARVERNA, so the two names echo each other. It was also carried by Queen Arwa of Yemen, who ruled for decades and is still remembered for building rather than conquering. A woman who stood in front.

Her life

She grew up between her grandmother's stone house and her mother's sewing machine. She studied architecture, then came back to Hebron to restore the old city. She dreams in Paris and lives at home on purpose. She's first to try every trend and edits it until it works for her real week. Her friends ask to borrow her clothes, and she usually says yes.

Face

Skin first: groomed brows, thin kohl line, rose-mauve lip. No heavy contour, no filters.

Hands

Three thin gold rings and her grandmother's small vintage watch. Short nails, nude or Jouri rose.

Bag

One structured mini bag in black or cream. On drop days, the ARVERNA kraft bag.

Shoes

Loafers, clean white sneakers, pointed slingbacks for the evening.

Posture

Mid-step, mid-laugh, looking straight at the camera. Never a pout, never a mirror.

Palette on her

Stone, qahwa, kohl. One rose or grape piece at a time.

How to bring her to life

Cast a real woman, not an AI influencer. Arwa is an archetype that a real local model or creator plays consistently: same face, same styling, every drop. A real face builds the trust a community needs. Keep a line-drawn illustration of her (sunglasses, low bun, trench collar) for stickers, the packaging insert and the WhatsApp profile.

08 · Identity system

Marks and devices

ARVERNA®

Wordmark

Keep the current wide wordmark; it's the brand's strongest asset. Use it bigger: at least 120 px wide in the site header, and full-bleed on bags and campaign posters. Shown here in the display face as a stand-in.

The Door A

A new monogram: the letter A drawn as the pointed stone doorway of Hebron's old city. The crossbar is missing on purpose, because the door stays open. The rose square is the threshold. Use it on labels, buttons, the favicon and the bag seal.

09 · Photography

Shoot it like you own it

Today most product photos come from suppliers, so customers can find the same images on other accounts. These five rules turn the imagery into something only ARVERNA has.

The stone wall packshot

Photograph every product against the same Hebron limestone wall, in the same morning light, at the same height. In a year the catalogue is recognisable from a single thumbnail. Choose one wall in the old city and treat it as the studio.

One face per drop

Arwa, or one Circle member, wears the whole drop. Put the model's height and size in the first caption line and on the product page.

Real places, named

Hebron old city, a Ramallah café, the Haifa seafront, a Bethlehem rooftop. Name the location in the caption. Stop using Istanbul trams and Paris boulevards that aren't ours.

Campaign grade

Monochrome with grain, with one rose element left in colour (a lip, a bag, a ribbon). It's the FW'26 poster look turned into a repeatable rule.

Movement over poses

Mid-step, turning, laughing with a friend. No mirror selfies, no heavy beauty filters, no forwarded WhatsApp files, no HEIC uploads.

10 · Voice

How she talks

Arabic first, English the way the customer actually mixes it in. Short sentences, specific facts, warm. Every line should sound like something Arwa would text a friend.

12 · Launch

The first 30 days

The FW'26 poster already says "this season marks a new chapter for ARVERNA." This plan makes that true.

Week 1
  • Approve the story, the tagline, the palette and the three typefaces.
  • Publish the bilingual About page with the name story.
  • Replace every line in the voice table on the site.
Week 2
  • Cast Arwa, then Leen, Tala and Jana (ideally from your own customers).
  • Choose the stone wall and shoot Drop 001 packshots against it.
  • Draw the Door A monogram properly with a designer and order the stickers.
Week 3
  • Open the Front Row: a WhatsApp broadcast list plus a Klaviyo signup form, with the member card.
  • Print the packaging insert and the Jouri-rose tissue.
  • Rebuild the Instagram highlights: Story, Size, Order, Delivery, Exchange, Front Row.
Week 4
  • Release Drop 001 "Stand in Front" on Thursday at 20:00, an hour earlier for members.
  • Post the first "One Set, Four Ways" Reel.
  • Open nominations for the first Muses.
