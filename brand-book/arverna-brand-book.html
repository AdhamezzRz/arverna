The brand story

It started with one question

Use it on the About page, in the launch Reel (as a voiceover) and on the packaging card. The Arabic version is written in light Palestinian dialect, the way the customer actually talks.

"Where's that from?"

Every girl has asked it. About a jacket she saw in a café, a dress at a friend's engagement, a look she saved at 2 a.m.

For a long time the answer was disappointing. Istanbul. Paris. A store that doesn't ship here. A three-week wait and a fit that's never quite right.

So we built a new answer: ARVERNA.

The looks you save, at your door in 2–3 days. New pieces every Thursday, picked by girls who live your life: campus at 8, coffee at 4, an engagement at 9. Real prices, real sizes, real people answering your messages.

And from now on you choose with us. Vote on the next drop. Show us how you wear it. Tag your friends.

So next time someone asks "where's that from?", you already know the answer.

Yara always

  • Says price, size and her height in the first 3 seconds.
  • Speaks dialect, mixing in English fashion words like her audience does.
  • Reposts customers who wore it better.
  • Replies to comments in her own voice, fast.

Yara never

  • Pretends an item fits when it doesn't.
  • Uses supplier photos or another brand's shoot.
  • Writes "Yes Please!!" or fake small caps.
  • Posts without tagging the product.
How to bring her to life

Cast a real local micro-creator (roughly 10–50K followers, 20–25, Hebron or Ramallah) as Yara on a 6-month contract. She posts on her own account and appears on @arvernacom every week. A real person builds trust faster than an invented or AI character, and her audience becomes yours. Use "Yara" as the series name ("Yara's Picks") so the format survives if the creator changes.

The brand icon

The A-Bow

A bow that spells A.
فيونكة أرڤيرنا

A rose satin ribbon tied into the letter A. It's a logo, a hair accessory, a sticker, a bag charm and a photo wall, all in one shape. Girls already love bows; this one has our initial in it, so every time she wears it, she's wearing ARVERNA.

  • Instantly ownable. No other brand in the market has a bow-letter mark.
  • On trend. Bows and coquette style are everywhere girls look right now.
  • Wearable. It becomes real merch: hair bows, bag charms, ribbon on every order.
  • Answers the big idea. When a friend asks "where's that from?", the bow already told her.
  • Works tiny and huge. A 32 px profile picture or a three-metre pop-up wall.

Identity system

Logo, type and the bubble

ARVERNA®

Logo

Keep the current wide wordmark; it already looks like a real fashion brand. What changes is the colour: cocoa on nude for bags, cocoa on cream for the site, cream on cocoa for social. Pair it with the A-Bow mark: the bow is the profile picture and favicon, the wordmark sits under it. Use it at least 120 px wide in the site header.

Where's that from?من وين هاد؟Relaxed fit · 160 ₪ · free delivery over 500 ₪قصّة مريحة · ١٦٠ ₪ · توصيل مجاني

Typography

Bodoni Moda Italic for campaign headlines (fashion-magazine glamour that contrasts with the wide logo). El Messiri for Arabic headlines. Figtree and Tajawal for everything else on the site. All free on Google Fonts. This replaces the five fonts the site uses now.

Where's it from?من وين؟

The speech bubble

The brand's graphic device, taken straight from the big idea. A rose speech bubble frames questions, prices, "NEW" and Yara's quotes. It also echoes WhatsApp and comments, where the community lives. Use it as stickers, bag seals, story frames and the favicon.

Photography

Bright, warm and real. Shoot on our own streets with Yara and real customers. One consistent cream or latte backdrop for product shots. Rose appears in every campaign (the A-Bow, a lip, a dress), with nude and cream around it and cocoa to ground it. Stop using supplier photos and mirror selfies from other studios.

Packaging

A nude bag with the cocoa logo and rope handle, tied shut with a rose satin ribbon. Rose tissue. A round "من وين؟" sticker as the seal. Inside, a card reading "Welcome to the girls 💌" with a QR code to the size guide and to join the Group Chat. The unboxing should be worth filming.
